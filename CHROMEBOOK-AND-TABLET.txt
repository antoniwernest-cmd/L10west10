CHROMEBOOK AND TABLET USE

Open the main index.html or the deployed website, then choose Passage Adventures.
Keep the complete Adventures-Deployment folder together when copying or deploying.

On smaller screens, use Story and Respond to switch views on the same page.
The text, picture and tools resize to the available screen without scrolling.
Mouse, trackpad and touch dragging are supported. Drag a card back to the bank to revise.
On tablets, portrait and landscape are supported. Typing mode reflows when the keyboard opens.
Listen starts reading; Stop ends it. Teacher voice settings apply in this browser.
Use the same picture ID and browser for saved work. Progress stays on that device.
Shuffle retains earned credit and answers. Teacher tools can export assessment to Excel.

Checks used desktop Chrome with emulated Chromebook/tablet screen sizes and touch input.
Physical Chromebooks, Android tablets and iPads were not available for hardware testing.
Speech requests and Stop were checked; voices and speakers depend on the actual device.
See CHROMEBOOK-TABLET-CHECKS.json for the test summary.

November 1 update: every story picture is on the Passage Adventures index.
Use A−, A+ and Fit in an activity to change text size. The preference saves in this browser.
See READING-LAYOUT-CHECKS.json for the latest layout and interaction checks.

Pre-A is available for every Passage Adventures story.
Cloze opens on the gaps. Story review unlocks after a correct response.
